# Prevention of Sexual Harassment

## Question 1: What kinds of behaviour cause sexual harassment?

Sexual harassment can include unwanted touching, sexual comments, jokes, gestures, or inappropriate conversations.  
It can also include sending unwanted messages, images, emails, or other sexual content.  
Repeated staring, asking for sexual favors, or making someone uncomfortable through unwanted behavior can also be harassment.  


## Question 2: What would you do in case you face or witness any incident or repeated incidents of such behaviour?

If I face or witness such behavior, I would not ignore it and would take the situation seriously.  
If it is safe, I would tell the person clearly that their behavior is inappropriate and should stop.   
I would report the incident to my manager, HR, or the appropriate POSH committee.  
If I witness someone being harassed, I would support the affected person and encourage them to report it.
