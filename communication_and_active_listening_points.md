# Communication and Active Listening

## 1. Strategies to Do Active Listening

- **Avoid getting distracted by your own thoughts**
- **Try not to interrupt the other person**
- **Use door openers**
- **Show that you are listening with body language**
- **Take notes when appropriate**
- **Paraphrase what others have said**

---

## 2. Key Points for Reflective Listening

- **Listen carefully**
- **Understand their meaning**
- **Reflect back**
- **Check your understanding**
- **Show empathy**
- **Avoid judging**
- **Clarify when needed**

---

## 3. Obstacles in Your Listening Process

- **Distractions**
- **Lack of concentration**
- **Interrupting**
- **Making assumptions**
- **Thinking about my response**
- **Lack of interest**

---

## 4. How to Improve Your Listening

- **Give the speaker my full attention**
- **Avoid using my phone or other distractions**
- **Do not interrupt while someone is speaking**
- **Ask questions when I don't understand something**
- **Take notes when necessary**
- **Repeat or summarize the main points**
- **Try to understand the speaker's feelings and point of view**
- **Practice active and reflective listening regularly**

---

## 5. Types of Communication

### 5.1 Passive Communication

- Do not clearly express thoughts or feelings
- Stay quiet to avoid conflict
- Avoid expressing opinions

### 5.2 Aggressive Communication

- Express thoughts in a rude or angry way
- Use harsh words
- Ignore other people's feelings
- Speak loudly or disrespectfully

### 5.3 Assertive Communication

- Clearly express thoughts and feelings
- Respect other people's thoughts and feelings
- Communicate clearly and confidently
- Handle disagreements respectfully

---

## 6. Passive Communication Style in Day-to-Day Life

- Afraid of hurting someone
- Want to avoid an argument
- Stay quiet when I disagree
- Avoid saying **"no"**
- Don't want to disappoint others
- Avoid expressing my opinion

---

## 7. Aggressive Communication Style in Day-to-Day Life

- When I am very angry
- When I am frustrated
- When someone repeatedly ignores me
- Speak loudly
- Use harsh words
- Become impatient
- React without thinking

---

## 8. Passive-Aggressive Communication Style in Day-to-Day Life

- When I am upset with someone
- When I don't want to directly express my feelings
- Give short replies
- Use sarcasm
- Make indirect comments
- Avoid talking to the person
- Give the silent treatment
- Use taunts

---

## 9. How to Make Your Communication Assertive

- **Speak clearly**
- **Use "I" statements**
- **Listen to others**
- **Stay calm**
- **Say no when necessary**
- **Avoid sarcasm and indirect comments**
- **Respect different opinions**
- **Think before responding**
- **Focus on the problem, not the person**
- **Practice regularly**
